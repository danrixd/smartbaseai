# Acme Analytics — Company Overview

Acme Analytics was founded in 2017 in Tel Aviv. The company builds real-time market analytics for mid-sized quantitative trading desks. As of 2024 the team is 42 people across research, engineering, and client ops.

## Products

- **Pulse** — streaming market-data aggregator that ingests L2 order books from 11 venues.
- **Atlas** — backtesting platform with a proprietary slippage model calibrated on 6 years of tick data.
- **Signal** — an alpha research IDE used internally and by three design-partner clients.
