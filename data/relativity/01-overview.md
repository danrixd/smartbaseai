# General Relativity — Research Notebook Overview

This vault holds notes, derivations, and annotated reading lists from the lab's ongoing work in general relativity (GR). It is intended as a private knowledge base for the research group, not for external publication.

## Scope

1. **Classical GR** — Schwarzschild, Kerr, and Reissner–Nordström solutions; black-hole thermodynamics; the Penrose process.
2. **Cosmology** — FLRW metric, inflation, late-time acceleration; ΛCDM and its tensions (H₀ tension, σ₈ tension).
3. **Gravitational waves** — LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA detections, waveform modeling, stochastic backgrounds, pulsar timing arrays (NANOGrav / EPTA / PPTA / InPTA).
4. **Modified gravity** — f(R), scalar-tensor, MOND phenomenology as a diagnostic, and how current tests constrain deviations from Einstein's equations.
