# Reading list — April 2026

Curated papers the group should know. ⭐ = read this first.

## Foundations
- ⭐ Wald, *General Relativity* (1984) — the textbook. Chapters 6, 11, 12 are mandatory for new group members.
- Poisson, *A Relativist's Toolkit* (2004) — formalism for hypersurfaces, Israel junction conditions. Needed for `02-hubble-tension.md`.
- Thorne, *300 Years of Gravitation* (1987) — historical grounding.

## Hubble tension
- ⭐ Riess et al. 2022 (SH0ES) — the distance-ladder result the whole tension pivots on.
- Verde, Treu & Riess 2019 — early summary of the tension.
- Di Valentino et al. 2021 — a comprehensive review with ~50 proposed resolutions. Useful for bibliography mining.
- Kamionkowski & Riess 2022 — the "new physics vs systematics" framing.

## Ringdown
- ⭐ Berti, Cardoso & Will 2006 — QNM tables, the canonical reference for Kerr modes.
- Giesler et al. 2019 — overtone claim on GW150914 (controversial).
- Cotesta et al. 2022 — the counterclaim; argues overtones aren't supported.
- Isi & Farr 2021 — careful Bayesian treatment.

## Numerical relativity
- Alcubierre, *Introduction to 3+1 Numerical Relativity* (2008).
- The Einstein Toolkit documentation (cactuscode.org).

## Group policies on reading

1. New papers cited in the weekly meeting must be logged here within 7 days.
2. ⭐ papers are required reading for all group members; un-starred entries are topic-specific.
3. For review articles, one person claims "reviewer of record" — they are responsible for giving a 15-minute summary at the next reading group.
