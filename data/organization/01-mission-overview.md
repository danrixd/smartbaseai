# Project Lunar Harbor — Mission Overview

> **Notice:** This document and the rest of the `organization` vault contain **fictional** mission data created for the SmartBaseAI demo. None of it reflects real NASA, ESA, CNSA, ISRO, or JAXA programs. Any resemblance to actual current Moon missions is coincidental and intended to illustrate retrieval over non-public corpora.

## Summary

Project Lunar Harbor is a fictional joint program to establish a permanently crewed south-pole outpost on the Moon by 2031. It spans three mission classes:

- **LH-Recon** — uncrewed robotic scouts (rovers + seismic probes). 4 missions launched, 2 more planned.
- **LH-Cargo** — robotic landers delivering habitat modules and ISRU (in-situ resource utilization) gear.
- **LH-Crew** — human-rated landers carrying a 4-person expedition crew for 45-day surface stays.

## Current phase

As of April 2026, Lunar Harbor is in the **Phase B-2 "Surface Readiness"** checkpoint. This phase runs from January 2026 to October 2026 and gates the first LH-Cargo-3 launch window.
